Tag quality

Tags are stored exactly as they arrive and checked at read time. A bad tag is flagged, not dropped, so fixing the taxonomy re-classifies old traffic instead of losing it.

The taxonomy

Every campaign name breaks into the same four parts, so a report can group by quarter or objective without a lookup table:

Rules

    • Internal links are never tagged. A UTM on an internal link would start a new attribution touch and erase the real source.
    • No personal data in UTMs: no names, emails, or IDs. They end up in URLs, logs, and screenshots.
